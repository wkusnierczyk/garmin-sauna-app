Sauna Watch Simulator

A browser model of the Connect IQ sauna app on a 5-button round watch. It starts on the watch face. Press START to open the activity list, scroll to Sauna, then pick Tracker or Program. Keys: ↑ ↓ for UP and DOWN, Enter for START, Esc for BACK. Q W S O L press the physical buttons directly, as in the Asteroids simulator. Holding UP for one second sends onMenu.

ButtonKeyNow

Actions fire on onKey, after release, as in the Asteroids model. UP waits for release so a long press can become onMenu without also scrolling.

Heart rate zones

Cutoffs follow Garmin's default method, a percentage of max heart rate. On the watch the app would read the user's own zones with UserProfile.getHeartRateZones(), so changes made in Garmin Connect carry over. Sauna-specific cutoffs can override them later.

    Heart rate is a simple model: it climbs in the heat, spikes on cold shock, then settles while resting. The sensor samples it once a second with noise. The watch shows a smoothed value that changes only when it has moved by about one beat, and drops a zone only 2 bpm below the cutoff.
